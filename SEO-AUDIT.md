# Puru Construction SEO audit — 15 October 2026

Canonical domain: https://puruconstruction.com/

## Findings
The production homepage, robots.txt and sitemap.xml returned HTTP 200. Existing company contact details, canonical URLs, Search Console verification and basic GeneralContractor/WebSite markup were present. Homepage service headings were generic and there were no dedicated service landing pages. An unrelated ELEVATE demo exists in pages/random_home.html (production returned 404).

## Changes
- Branded homepage title and H1, accurate location and service descriptions.
- Four distinct, internally linked service pages: road construction, bridge and culvert construction, drainage construction, civil construction and supply.
- Connected GeneralContractor, WebSite and WebPage entities; legal and alternate company names; factual service catalog. Service and breadcrumb structured data on service pages.
- Root sitemap.xml with seven canonical page URLs and six original company images; meaningful modification dates on changed pages only.
- Root robots.txt permits crawling and points to the canonical sitemap. No unsupported noindex directive.
- Permanent /index.html redirect to /; existing www-to-apex redirects preserved.
- Demo pages excluded from deployment, with HTML and HTTP noindex safeguards. Internal documentation excluded from deployment.
- Preserved Search Console verification and existing contact information. No invented awards, reviews, registration, opening hours or geographic coverage.

## Search targets
Primary: Puru Construction & Suppliers Pvt. Ltd.; Puru Construction; Puru Construction and Suppliers Pvt. Ltd.
Secondary: construction company Sarlahi; construction company Lalbandi; road construction Sarlahi; bridge and culvert construction Sarlahi; drainage construction Sarlahi; civil construction and supply Sarlahi.
Use these naturally in relevant content. Do not add keyword stuffing, hidden text or a meta keywords tag.

## Owner-dependent work
In Google Search Console, confirm the property https://puruconstruction.com/, submit sitemap.xml, and inspect/request indexing of the homepage and service pages. The existing verification meta tag was retained. Keep Google Business Profile company name, address, phone and website consistent. Add only genuine company project descriptions, approved project photographs, verified credentials and authentic customer reviews as they become available.

## Measurement
Monitor Search Console indexing, crawl errors, branded query impressions/clicks and service queries after deployment. Google decides crawl timing, indexing and rankings; no implementation guarantees a first-place result.
