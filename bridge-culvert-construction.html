Bridge and culvert construction in Sarlahi

Puru Construction & Suppliers Pvt. Ltd. focuses on bridges, culverts and crossing works as part of its public infrastructure activity in Sarlahi, Nepal.

Connecting routes and communities

Bridges and culverts serve different crossing needs. A bridge carries a route across an obstacle; a culvert provides a passage for water beneath a road or embankment. Each requires a clearly defined scope based on the site and project documentation.

Information for a crossing enquiry

Share the following details so our team can understand your requirements:

  • Project location and the type of crossing required.
  • Available engineering drawings, specifications and scope of work.
  • Road access, surrounding drainage and any timing or site constraints.

Coordination with the wider project

Crossing works often connect with road approaches and drainage. Contact the company to discuss the supplied project requirements and how construction and supply activity will be coordinated.
