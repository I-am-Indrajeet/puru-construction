## Visual direction

Use the supplied Puru emblem as a prominent, recognizable brand asset. The mark is deep green with warm ivory construction/building shapes and a small red crane accent. Keep the exact legal name as selectable live text beside the mark; do not recreate the company name inside an image.

Avoid heavy all-caps UI, oversized editorial headlines, excessive tracking, dark cinematic overlays, ornamental technical grids, animated tickers, and attention-seeking motion.

## Page structure

1. **Header** — visible Puru emblem and exact company name at the left; compact navigation for Company, Services, Photos, and Contact; a clear phone action. On mobile, retain a legible logo/name lockup and accessible menu.
2. **Introduction** — a plain-language headline describing public-infrastructure construction and supply in Sarlahi. Pair it with a supplied, genuine company/team/equipment photograph. Label it as a company photograph; do not imply the image depicts a named contract.
3. **Company overview** — a concise description of the business, its Raniganj/Lalbandi-17 base, and coordination with public-sector stakeholders. Include only grounded facts.
4. **Services** — four concise categories: Road works; Bridge and crossing works; Drainage; Related civil construction and supply. Describe the company’s focus without claiming every subtype is a completed project.
5. **How the company works** — a short, modest summary of communication, coordination, mobilization, and site work. Do not make quality, safety, delivery-time, or engineering guarantees.
6. **Company photographs** — a small, orderly gallery of supplied original photographs (team/equipment, field tractor, plant operations, equipment yard, and site tradition). Use factual captions such as “Company team and equipment” or “Plant operations.” Do not use AI-generated illustrative scenes as portfolio evidence.
7. **Leadership** — if shown, use the supplied photo and the verified name/role of Managing Director Punar Adhikari. Any other named staff should be limited to the supplied, previously approved facts; do not invent biographies. Team functions must be clearly labelled as functions rather than named people.
8. **Contact and location** — show the confirmed phone/email as working links and Raniganj, Lalbandi-17 as the office area. Keep the map link/embed clear that it locates the Raniganj area, not a verified door address. Link to the existing Google Business Profile where available.
9. **Footer** — company name/logo, copyright year, Privacy Policy, Terms, and cookie notice controls.

## Image and truthfulness rules

- Prefer the actual company photos supplied by the owner for the hero and gallery.
- Remove generated bridge, road, culvert, and site-coordination imagery from any section that could be mistaken for completed Puru work. If an illustrative scene must remain, label it explicitly as illustrative and keep it outside the photo gallery.
- Avoid captions with invented project locations, contract identifiers, completion status, client names, or dates.
- Give every informative photo accurate alt text. Decorative logo images can use empty alt text because the adjacent live text provides the brand name.

## Interaction and accessibility

- Use familiar links and buttons with visible focus states and clear labels.
- Keep motion subtle and optional; support `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Ensure mobile navigation and photo layouts work by touch and keyboard.
- Keep text readable over all photos; avoid overlaying essential copy on busy areas.
- Preserve the existing privacy, terms, and cookie-notice behavior.

## Acceptance checks

- The supplied logo is clearly visible at first glance on desktop and mobile, with the official name next to it.
- The landing view uses an authentic company photograph and plainly states the actual business focus and location.
- No generated image or decorative claim is presented as proof of a completed project.
- Phone, email, map, privacy, terms, and cookie notice remain usable.
- Company facts and services stay within the evidence supplied by the owner.
- Desktop/mobile layout, accessible navigation, reduced motion, and production HTTPS remain functional.
